# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want QdrantBar's community to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussion on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing or spam.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/fr3on/qdrantbar/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer, [@fr3on](https://github.com/fr3on), directly. Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
